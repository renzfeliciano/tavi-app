# Tavi audit: Settings, documents, payments (2026-10-12)

Read-only review of the code on branch `audit/settings-payments-withholding`. "Confirmed" means seen in the code; nothing here was reproduced against production data. Unit and component tests (1,009) were run; integration and Playwright tests were not (no test database or browser in the audit sandbox).

## Summary

The foundations are strong: integer money, one calculation function, stored statuses derived by one pure rule, org-scoped queries with composite foreign keys, audited commands, invoice lock before payment, gapless numbering, a safe logo pipeline. The gaps are at the edges of the payment story: no way to correct a paid invoice, no installment/deposit concept, withholding is only a number on a payment row, issued documents show the live business profile, and payment recording has no server-side idempotency.

## Findings

| ID | Feature | Issue | Evidence | Severity | Type | Recommendation |
|----|---------|-------|----------|----------|------|----------------|
| F-01 | Documents / Settings | Issued invoices and quotes print the **current** business name, TIN, address, tax registration and logo. Changing Settings rewrites how old documents look (scenario 10). The customer, lines and payment instructions are already snapshotted; the seller is not. | `invoices/schema.ts` (no seller snapshot); `getLetterheadForSharedDocument` (portal, quote and invoice send); `invoices/[id]/pdf/route.tsx`, `quotes/[id]/pdf/route.tsx` call `getBusinessProfile` | High | Confirmed | Add a nullable `seller_snapshot` jsonb written when a document is issued; render from it, fall back to live for older rows. Backfill needs your OK (it freezes today's profile). |
| F-02 | Invoices | A part-paid or paid invoice cannot be corrected: `PARTIALLY_PAID` and `PAID` allow no event; void and cancel require zero payments. The only route is voiding the payments first, which erases real cash from the books. | `invoices/domain/transitions.ts`; `closeInvoice` in `corrections.ts` | High | Confirmed (missing capability) | Credit notes and refunds as agreed. |
| F-03 | Payments | Recording a payment has no idempotency. The UI disables the button while pending and the invoice row lock prevents overpaying, but a network retry or second tab with the same amount records a second payment and a second receipt number while balance remains (scenario 8). | `recordPayment` in `payments/application/payments.ts`; no key column in `payments/schema.ts` | Medium | Confirmed (server side) | Client-generated idempotency key, unique per business; a repeat returns the first result. |
| F-04 | Payments / reports | `invoices.amount_paid_minor` holds cash **plus** withholding, but is named and commented as "paid". Portal balance, attention list and reports read it as paid. Cash totals are computed separately from payments (correct). | `settlement.ts`, `invoices/schema.ts`, `invoices/application/reports.ts` | Medium | Confirmed (naming/semantics) | Keep the column for compatibility, rename in code and UI to "settled", and show cash and withholding separately. |
| F-05 | Withholding | Withholding is a number on a payment row. No certificate number, period, rate, received-or-pending status. Withholding with no cash still issues a payment receipt number. | `payments/schema.ts`; `parsePaymentInput` | Medium | Missing requirement | Separate withholding credit record as agreed; backfill from existing `withheld_minor`. | F-06 | Quotes / invoices | No deposit or installment schedule. Conversion copies lines only; payments are free-form so there is no "amount due now". | `invoices/application/conversion.ts` | High | Missing requirement | Schedule on the quote, copied at conversion, editable on the draft; shown on the statement. |

| F-08 | Settings | Logo overflow: three different sizing rules (preview, document, PDF) and no preview before saving. | Fixed on this branch: shared `fitLogo`, preview before save, extreme-ratio warning. | Medium | Confirmed, fixed | Done; e2e not yet run. |
| F-09 | Settings | Business profile: validated per market, audited by field name, owner/admin only, transactional with a row lock; logo re-encoded by sharp, SVG refused, 2 MB cap. No defect found. | `business-profile.ts`, `settings/business/actions.ts`, `files/application/logo.ts` | n/a | Reviewed | Numbering, invoicing registration, tax rates and team pages still need the same depth of review (listed under open items). |

## Not yet audited

Numbering, invoicing registration, tax-rate and team pages in depth; quote totals parity between editor, portal and PDF (single calculation function by design, parity not independently tested here); e-invoice export; reports arithmetic; email sending limits; security headers; the integration and e2e suites (not run).

## Proposed implementation (needs your approval)

All changes additive, behind migrations rehearsed on a Neon copy; nothing touches production.

1. Idempotency key on payments (F-03). Low risk.
2. Seller snapshot on issued documents (F-01). Needs approval for the backfill.
3. Withholding credits table plus backfill (F-04, F-05). Needs approval; tax treatment unconfirmed.
4. Installment schedule: quote, copy at conversion, editable draft, statement table with "amount due now" (F-06).
5. Credit notes and refunds (F-02), the largest piece.

Each step: tests first, lint, typecheck, unit/component run, integration run on the test branch.

01 What changed

Now
Redrawn

A head you can read. A wide dome with large eyes, a small mouth and light cheeks. The old one had no face to read, so every expression was a pair of dots.

Outlined, not filled. A white head on an ink body, with one ink line weight throughout. It keeps its shape on bond paper, on white, and at 20px.

Same rules as before. Flat colour, no limbs, no mouth-heavy faces, violet only where ink would go: the pad and the imprint.

03 How it is alive

  1. Blinks every few seconds, each one out of step with the others.
  2. Breathes. A 1.5% swell in the body, slow enough to miss unless you look.
  3. Watches the cursor. The pupils and head lean toward it, a few pixels at most. On touch screens it glances around on its own.
  4. Reacts. A tap presses it down and the imprint draws in. Hover makes it hop once.

Motion stays small and never covers content. Money, totals and statuses never animate.

With "reduce motion" turned on, it is a still drawing: no blink, no follow, no hop.

This changes today's rule of "animates only on request, once". The new rule is: idle motion is allowed, large motion only for real moments (sent, approved, paid).

04 On invoices

The business's logo and name still lead. The Stamp appears in the footer credit on every document, and as a PAID or APPROVED imprint once a document reaches that moment. The imprint stays clear of line items and totals. In the PDF the mascot is still, as a PDF cannot move.
